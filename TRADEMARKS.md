# Editkin name and visual identity

The GPL covers the program source. It does not grant ownership of the Editkin name, logo, official domains, signing identity, or update channel. A fork may accurately describe itself as compatible with Editkin or derived from Editkin, but should use its own product name, icon, download site, signing identity, and update channel so users are not confused about its origin. Keep notices required by the code and asset licenses.

The project includes Editkin icon files to make source builds possible. The maintainer permits their use in unmodified source builds and accurate screenshots of Editkin. Separate permission is required to present a modified fork as an official Editkin release. This policy does not restrict rights granted by the GPL for program code, and does not claim a registered trademark.
